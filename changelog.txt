Version: 1.0.2
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Banking turns: drones visibly roll into their turns. The wings narrow, the lowered wing dips, and the plane slides toward the inside of the turn. Can be turned off with the new "Banking turns" map setting.
  Changes:
    - Renamed to "WePlay: Long Haul Logistics Drones".
    - Launch pads no longer show the "not in a logistic network" warning, since pads connect to each other at any range.
    - The pad panel now shows whether the pad is inside a robot network.
    - "Show drones on the map" is now off by default.
  Graphics:
    - New mod thumbnail with the cargo biplanes.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.1
Date: 2026-11-10
  Changes:
    - Removed the drone route lines from the map.
    - New map setting "Show drones on the map" (on by default) to show or hide the drone dots in map view.
  Bugfixes:
    - Drones with nowhere to dock no longer vanish when their pad's inventory is full; they drop next to the pad instead.
    - Cargo waiting on a runway no longer vanishes if its destination is removed and the pad is full; it drops next to the pad instead.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-11-10
  Features:
    - Long haul launch pad: a 5x5 rotatable buffer chest with 80 slots that launches cargo drones to other pads, with no range limit.
    - Requests use the vanilla logistics picker. Anything the pad's local robots can't supply is flown in from other pads.
    - Cargo drones with loaded and empty models, runway takeoffs, wide banking turns and lined-up landings.
    - Drone slots like a roboport: 20 drones per pad, up to 40 with research. Mined pads give their drones back.
    - Fair dispatch: drones are shared round-robin across every pad that needs something, with a 0-255 priority per pad.
    - Rebalancing: outposts keep 2 drones and send the rest home to the nearest hub.
    - Spread loads / Fill drones switch per pad. Payload is by item weight: 100 kg, 250 kg or 500 kg with research.
    - Power: 500 kW idle, 1 MW during launches. Loaded launches pause without power; empty drones can always fly home.
    - Circuit network: set requests from combinators, and read available, at-pad, inbound and network drone counts on any signals.
    - Drones and routes show on the map, colored by trip type.
    - Research: Long haul logistics, plus Long haul cargo capacity 1 and 2.
    - Mod setting to switch fancy flight paths off for straight pad-to-pad flights.
    - Works per surface, so it's compatible with Space Age, Space Exploration and other multi-surface mods.
